Assembly order

  1. Cut the profile and diffuser to length and fit the end caps.
  2. Make a recess spacer for each end and check it sits flush with the tops of the side walls.
  3. Slide a tapped strip into the T-slot cavity at each end so its hole sits 4.25 from the end.
  4. Lay the spacer in the recess, fold the arm back over it and fit the two screws. Their heads sit below the arm's top face.
  5. Mark the shelf screw line 65 from the shelf's front edge and the screw positions from the setting-out table. Drill the pilot holes.
  6. Hold the light up, with the arms resting against the shelf, and screw the hinge bodies to the shelf.

Check first

  • Slot, cavity and recess sizes are measured from photos against a ruler, to about ±0.3. Dry-fit the strip, spacer and screws first, and trim the T-slot screw so it doesn't touch the web.
  • Use low-head screws: the arm's top face rests against the shelf, so nothing may stand above it.
  • Southco's drawing note also recommends low-profile cap screws where the head must sit below the surface.

Southco small, flat: fit notes

  • The bar is 19 mm wide inside the slots so it rests on both end lips, then widens to about 26 mm for the 15 mm stud spacing.
  • Trim the bar-side studs to 8–9 mm or they reach past the underside of the shelf.
  • Bore the nut recesses in the spacer's top face so each nut sits about 7 mm from the bottom.
  • A small bolt through the bar inside the U stops it sliding out sideways.
  • A 900 mm run needs about 0.06 Nm per hinge, well within the 0.25 Nm maximum.

Southco medium, flat: fit notes

  • Southco doesn't supply the screws. Use M4 pan or cheese heads that sit inside the Ø8.9 × 2.5 counterbores.
  • The torque screw is on the leaf face, pointing down, so you can adjust it with the light in place.
  • A 900 mm run needs about 0.05 Nm per hinge, near the bottom of the 0.8 Nm range, so adjust in small turns.
  • The hinge is bigger than the small one, so the bar's wide end grows to 40 mm along the profile and the spacer to 54 mm.

Southco small, folded: fit notes

  • Before committing, fold a hinge shut by hand and check it closes a full 180°. Southco doesn't state the travel.
  • The shelf-side studs are glued, so the hinges can't be removed from the shelf afterwards. The profile still unscrews from the plate.
  • Set the knuckle about 2 mm behind the rail. That puts the plate's stud holes clear of the profile's front edge for the nuts.
  • A 900 mm run needs about 0.05 Nm per hinge, well within the 0.25 Nm maximum.

ST-7A3, end mounted: fit notes

  • Order two ST-7A3-15ST. Type T isn't handed. Southco says a pair must face opposite ways, which they do when fitted at opposite ends.
  • The 15ST at 0.17 Nm is the lightest. The 30ST at 0.34 Nm works but is stiff to move, and the torque can't be adjusted down.
  • -33 is natural, as-cast zinc. The black finishes are custom or bulk orders. The hinges sit behind the rail, so they're mostly out of sight.
  • The folded arm sits against the underside of the shelf, so screw the arms to the profile first, then screw the bodies up into the shelf.
  • At rest the arm is folded back beside the hinge body. Southco doesn't state the travel, so check the arm folds that far before cutting anything.
  • The back of the profile is recessed about 1.4 mm between its side walls, so a 1.5 mm aluminium spacer goes under each arm to keep it flat. The rear arm screw threads into the spacer and mainly stops the arm twisting. The T-slot screw carries the load.
  • Run the LED cable out through the end cap under one of the hinges, where it moves least as the light tilts.

Kipp, folded: fit notes

  • Order the version without guide tabs. The 6–10 mm tabs won't fit this profile's slot.
  • Set the hinge axis about 4.75 mm in front of the profile's front edge so the leaf's holes sit over the T-slot.
  • Leave a small gap between the 20 mm knuckle and the back of the rail.
  • A 900 mm run needs only about 0.03 Nm per hinge, near the bottom of the range, so adjust in small turns.
  • The back of the profile is recessed about 1.4 mm between its side walls. Fit a 1.5 mm aluminium spacer under the leaf so it sits flat, and use a 1.8 mm strip in the 2.0 mm T-slot cavity.

Assumptions

  • Clip walls about 3 mm thick.
  • Profile back-face sizes are measured from photos against a ruler, to about ±0.3 mm.
  • One hinge set is shown on a 120 mm length of profile. An 800–900 mm run uses two, about 180 mm in from each end, on one straight axis.
  • Profile weight of about 0.4 kg per metre, used for the torque figures.
  • ST-7A3 type T geometry from Southco drawing J-ST-7A3-T: axis 3.5 from the mounting face, 7 mm arm centred on the axis, arm holes 8 and 17 from the axis, body holes 8 from the axis and 3.55 and 12.55 from the arm side.
