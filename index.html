End view

  • Shelf-side studs trimmed to about 10 and glued into Ø6 holes (dashed).
  • Profile-side studs pass through the plate in front of the profile, with nuts underneath. Trim them to about 7.
  • Plate fixed to a short tapped strip in the T-slot with two M3 screws.
  • The 5 mm axis offset is read from the Southco drawing. Check it on the part.

End view

  • Axis 4.75 in front of the profile's front edge, so the leaf holes sit over the T-slot.
  • Leaves 27 from the axis and 8 thick.

Parts to make

Flat bar

Southco · plan
  • 3 mm aluminium, filed to slide in the 3 mm slots.
  • The narrow part is 18.5 wide so it passes the 19 mm slots and rests on both end lips.
  • Ø5.5 holes for the hinge studs. Ø2.7 hole for an M2.5 retaining bolt, 4 off the centreline.
  • The dashed outline shows where the clip block sits.

Spacer

Southco · plan
  • Hardwood, 10 thick. The rail face is on the right.
  • Ø5.5 through holes for the studs, each with a Ø11 × 3 deep nut recess in the top face (dashed).
  • Two Ø4 holes for wood screws into the shelf.

Tapped strip

Kipp · plan
  • Aluminium, about 2 thick. Measure the T-slot cavity first: the supplier's drawing suggests about 2 mm behind 1.3 mm lips.
  • Drill 2.5 and tap M3. Slide it in from the end of the profile.
  • Fix with M3 screws and washers through the Kipp counterbores.
  • For the folded Southco, make it 30 long with the two M3 holes 16 apart.

Adapter plate

Southco folded · plan
  • 3 mm aluminium. The right-hand edge lines up with the hinge axis, toward the rail.
  • Ø5.5 holes for the hinge studs, which pass in front of the profile with nuts underneath.
  • Ø3.2 holes for M3 screws into the tapped strip.

Southco, flat: fit notes

  • The bar is 19 mm wide inside the slots so it rests on both end lips, then widens to about 26 mm for the 15 mm stud spacing.
  • Trim the bar-side studs to 8–9 mm or they reach past the underside of the shelf.
  • Bore the nut recesses in the spacer's top face so each nut sits about 7 mm from the bottom.
  • A small bolt through the bar inside the U stops it sliding out sideways.
  • A 900 mm run needs about 0.06 Nm per hinge, well within the 0.25 Nm maximum.

Southco, folded: fit notes

  • Before committing, fold a hinge shut by hand and check it closes a full 180°. Southco doesn't state the travel.
  • The shelf-side studs are glued, so the hinges can't be removed from the shelf afterwards. The profile still unscrews from the plate.
  • Set the knuckle about 2 mm behind the rail. That puts the plate's stud holes clear of the profile's front edge for the nuts.
  • A 900 mm run needs about 0.05 Nm per hinge, well within the 0.25 Nm maximum.

Kipp, folded: fit notes

  • Order the version without guide tabs. The 6–10 mm tabs won't fit this profile's slot.
  • Set the hinge axis about 4.75 mm in front of the profile's front edge so the leaf's holes sit over the T-slot.
  • Leave a small gap between the 20 mm knuckle and the back of the rail.
  • A 900 mm run needs only about 0.03 Nm per hinge, near the bottom of the range, so adjust in small turns.
  • Measure the T-slot cavity height before cutting the strip. It looks closer to 2 mm than 2.5 mm.

Assumptions

  • Clip walls about 3 mm thick.
  • Profile T-slot sizes are scaled from the supplier's cross-section drawing.
  • One hinge set is shown on a 120 mm length of profile. An 800–900 mm run uses two, about 180 mm in from each end, on one straight axis.
  • Profile weight of about 0.4 kg per metre, used for the torque figures.
