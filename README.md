# FREDDE LED hinge

A tilting LED light under the top shelf of an IKEA FREDDE desk. A black LEDBRITE ALP3025-B aluminium profile hangs behind the shelf's front rail on two adjustable-friction hinges, so it can be angled to throw light forward onto the desk.

**View the design online: <https://covcodelab.github.io/ikea-fredde-led-lighting/>**

The same page is `index.html` in this repository. It contains:

- interactive 3D models of all five hinge layouts, with a tilt slider
- a comparison table
- a parts list with sources and prices
- dimensioned end views of all five assemblies and drawings of the parts to make

The 3D models load three.js from a CDN, so they need an internet connection.

The medium Southco is the easiest of the flat layouts to build: no studs to trim and no nut recesses. The folded Southco hangs highest and needs no clip block, but only works if the hinge folds a full 180°, which Southco doesn't state. Check it by hand first. The Kipp needs the fewest made parts. The end-mounted ST-7A3 hangs the profile highest of all, about 8 mm below the rail, with small fixed-torque hinges.

## Open checks

- For the ST-7A3, check the arm folds back beside the body at rest. Its rear screw goes into a narrow strip in the rear L-slot, which has a lip on one edge only, so tighten it gently.
- For the folded Southco, fold a hinge shut by hand and check it closes a full 180°.
- Measure the profile's slots before cutting strips. Scaled from the supplier drawing: T-slot opening about 3.4 mm, cavity about 10.1 × 2.9 mm behind 1.5 mm lips; rear L-slots opening about 4.0 mm, cavity about 5.0 × 2.9 mm, lip on the outer edge only.
- Measure the real profile. The supplier cross-section and the LEDBRITE listing give different sizes.
- Check the Southco stud length against the bar and spacer before trimming.
