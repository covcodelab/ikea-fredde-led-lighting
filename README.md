# FREDDE LED hinge

A tilting LED light under the top shelf of an IKEA FREDDE desk. A black LEDBRITE ALP3025-B aluminium profile hangs behind the shelf's front rail on two friction hinges, so it can be angled to throw light forward onto the desk.

**View the design online: <https://covcodelab.github.io/ikea-fredde-led-lighting/>**

The same page is `index.html` in this repository. It contains:

- interactive 3D models of all four hinge layouts, with a tilt slider
- a comparison table
- a parts list with sources and prices
- dimensioned end views of all four assemblies and drawings of the parts to make

The 3D models load three.js from a CDN, so they need an internet connection.

The folded Southco only works if the hinge folds a full 180°, which Southco doesn't state. Check it by hand first. The Kipp and both ST-7A3 layouts need only a tapped strip and a recess spacer per hinge. The end-mounted ST-7A3 hangs the profile highest of all, about 8 mm below the rail, with small fixed-torque hinges. The rail-mounted ST-7A3 uses the same hinges and parts but screws them to the steel rail instead of the shelf: it hangs about 23 mm below the rail, and its tilt opens the hinge towards flat, a movement Southco's drawing confirms.

## Open checks

- For the ST-7A3, check the arm folds back beside the body at rest. Its rear screw threads into the recess spacer and mainly stops the arm twisting.
- For the folded Southco, fold a hinge shut by hand and check it closes a full 180°.
- Profile back face, measured from photos (±0.3 mm): side walls stand about 1.4 mm above the T-slot hooks, so a 1.5 mm recess spacer goes under any flat leaf, adapter plate or arm. T-slot opening about 7.2 mm, cavity about 9.8 × 2.0 mm under lips about 1.5 mm thick. Dry-fit the tapped strips, recess spacers and screws before final assembly.
- For the rail-mounted ST-7A3, measure the rail's back wall. Tap M3 if it is about 1.5 mm or more, otherwise use low-flange rivet nuts. Check a 2.5 mm hex key can reach the rail screws from behind.
- Measure the real profile. The supplier cross-section and the LEDBRITE listing give different sizes.
- Check the Southco stud lengths before trimming: about 10 on the shelf side and 7 on the profile side.
