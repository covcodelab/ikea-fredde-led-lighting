# FREDDE LED hinge

A tilting LED light under the top shelf of an IKEA FREDDE desk. A black LEDBRITE ALP3025-B aluminium profile hangs behind the shelf's front rail on two adjustable-friction hinges, so it can be angled to throw light forward onto the desk.

Open `index.html` in a browser for the full design. It contains:

- interactive 3D models of both hinge options, with a tilt slider
- a comparison table
- a parts list with sources and prices
- dimensioned end views of both assemblies and drawings of the parts to make

The 3D models load three.js from a CDN, so they need an internet connection.

## Options

| | Southco E6 | Kipp K0440 |
|---|---|---|
| Part | E6-10-101-20 | K0440.56181800 (no guide tabs) |
| Torque | up to 0.25 Nm | 0–5 Nm |
| Mounting | flat bar through the clip-wall slots, hinge below it, fixed leaf on a 10 mm hardwood spacer against the rail | hinge folded under the shelf, lower leaf screwed to a tapped strip in the profile's T-slot |
| Profile bottom below the rail | about 17 | about 22 |
| Parts to make | flat bar, spacer, trimmed studs | one tapped strip |
| Torque needed per hinge (900 mm run) | about 0.06 Nm | about 0.03 Nm |

The Southco hangs about 5 mm higher. The Kipp is simpler to build.

## Open checks

- Measure the profile's T-slot cavity height before cutting the Kipp strip. The supplier drawing suggests about 2 mm.
- Measure the real profile. The supplier cross-section and the LEDBRITE listing give different sizes.
- Check the Southco stud length against the bar and spacer before trimming.
